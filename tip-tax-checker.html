How this works

What is the No Tax on Tips deduction?

Section 224 of the tax code (One Big Beautiful Bill Act) lets workers in tipped occupations deduct up to $25,000 of qualified tips for tax years 2025–2028. It is claimed on IRS Schedule 1-A and stacks on top of the standard deduction — you can claim it whether or not you itemize.

Who loses part of it?

The deduction shrinks once your modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) passes $150,000 ($300,000 if married filing jointly): it is reduced by $100 for each full $1,000 over the limit, and reaches zero at $400,000 ($550,000 joint).

Do I still pay other taxes on tips?

Yes. This deduction only reduces federal income tax. Social Security and Medicare taxes still apply to all tip income, and most states still tax tips. You must still report your tips.

Is this exact?
