How this estimate is built +
  • Federal brackets & standard deduction (2026): IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32 — single: 10% to $12,400, 12% to $50,400, 22% to $105,700, 24% to $201,775, 32% to $256,225, 35% to $640,600, 37% above; standard deduction $16,100 single / $32,200 married filing jointly.
  • Self-employment tax: 15.3% (12.4% Social Security + 2.9% Medicare) on 92.35% of net profit. Social Security portion stops at the 2026 wage base of $184,500 (SSA), reduced by any W-2 wages. Half of SE tax is deducted before income tax. The extra 0.9% Additional Medicare Tax over $200,000 ($250,000 joint) is not modeled.
  • Federal tax is marginal: we tax your total income, then subtract the tax on your W-2 wages alone, so the result is the tax caused by the hustle itself.
  • Tips deduction: qualified tips are deductible up to $25,000 (OBBBA, tax years 2025–2028), capped at net business profit for the self-employed, phasing out by $100 per $1,000 of income over $150,000 ($300,000 joint). Tips still owe SE tax.
  • QBI deduction: 20% of qualified business income (profit minus half of SE tax and health premiums), capped at 20% of taxable income before the deduction. Above the §199A threshold ($201,750 single / $403,500 joint, Rev. Proc. 2025-32), additional wage/SSTB limits can apply and are not modeled.
  • Mileage: IRS standard mileage — 72.5¢/mile Jan–Jun 2026 (Notice 2026-10, IR-2025-128) and 76¢/mile Jul–Dec 2026 (Announcement 2026-11, IR-2026-29). Keep a mileage log; the IRS requires one.
  • Home office (simplified): $5 per sq ft up to 300 sq ft (IRS simplified option). Health premiums: self-employed health insurance deduction — reduces income tax, not SE tax, capped at net profit.
  • State tax: a simplified flat rate applied to net profit, chosen as a representative mid-range rate for your state. Real state taxes use their own brackets, deductions and sometimes city taxes — treat this as a planning approximation only.
  • Quarterly dates (2026 Form 1040-ES): April 15, June 15, September 15, 2026 and January 15, 2027.
  • International: UK — gov.uk (Personal Allowance £12,570 with the £100,000 taper; 20/40/45% bands; excludes National Insurance and Scottish rates). France — service-public.fr barème 2026 per part fiscale (single part assumed; excludes décote and social contributions). Canada — canada.ca federal brackets 2026 with the Basic Personal Amount credit ($16,452, phasing to $14,829); excludes provincial tax, CPP and EI. Australia — ato.gov.au resident rates 2026–27 plus the 2% Medicare levy; excludes the Low Income Tax Offset and HECS/HELP.
