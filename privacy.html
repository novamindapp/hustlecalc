Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

HustleCalc is a side-hustle tax calculator that runs entirely in your web browser. This page explains, in plain language, what data is involved when you use it.

The numbers you type in

Your income, expenses and other figures are calculated on your own device. They are never sent to us — we run no server that receives or stores your financial figures, and there are no user accounts. Some preferences (such as your checklist progress) are saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only. The same applies if you use the Pro tools: your saved scenarios and your mileage & expense log live only in that local storage and are never sent to us.

License keys (Pro)

If you buy HustleCalc Pro and enter a license key, the key is sent over an encrypted connection to our verification endpoint, which checks it with Payhip, the payment platform that issued it. The check returns the key's purchase date, which is used to calculate its 30-day validity. The key and its expiry date are then stored in your browser's local storage so Pro stays unlocked on that device. Our endpoint does not keep a copy of your key or your figures.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Payhip and its payment partners (such as PayPal). We never see or store your card or bank details. Payhip's own privacy policy applies to the checkout and to the email address you give them for delivery.

Advertising

We show ads provided by Adsterra. Ad networks like Adsterra may use cookies or similar identifiers to serve and measure ads. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings — the calculator will still work.

Other third parties

  • Vercel — hosts this website and may process standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to deliver pages securely.
  • Google Fonts — delivers the typeface used on this site.

We do not run our own analytics trackers, and we do not sell personal data — largely because we don't collect it.

Your choices

You can erase everything this site stores on your device at any time by clearing site data in your browser. Note that this also removes your Pro activation on that device; you can re-enter your license key to restore it while the key is still within its 30 days.

Children

HustleCalc is a tax-planning tool for adults earning self-employment income. It is not directed at children.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Questions: contact us through our store page on Payhip, or reply to any Payhip receipt email.
