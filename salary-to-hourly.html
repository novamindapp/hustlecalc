How to convert a salary to an hourly wage

Divide your pay by the number of hours you work in that period. For a yearly salary:

Hourly wage = Annual salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year)

The 2,080-hour rule

A standard full-time job is 40 hours a week for 52 weeks — 2,080 work hours a year. So a quick estimate for any salary is simply the salary divided by 2,080. Example: $60,000 ÷ 2,080 = $28.85 an hour.

What if I get unpaid time off?

If you take unpaid weeks off, lower the "weeks per year" in the calculator (for example 50 instead of 52). Your salary is then spread over fewer hours, so your true hourly rate is higher than the 2,080 estimate.

Comparing a job offer?

Hourly pay makes offers easy to compare — but remember benefits, paid time off and overtime rules can be worth several dollars an hour on top of the base rate.

Questions, answered

How much is $60,000 a year per hour?

At 40 hours a week for 52 weeks (2,080 hours a year), $60,000 a year is $28.85 an hour before taxes — $1,153.85 a week, or $5,000 a month.

How is overtime pay calculated?

For non-exempt employees in the US, overtime is paid at 1.5 times your regular rate for hours over 40 in a week (some employers pay double). At $20 an hour, each overtime hour pays $30. Turn on "Add overtime pay" above to see what it adds up to over a year.

Is overtime taxed at a higher rate?

No — that's a myth. Overtime is taxed at the same rates as regular pay. A bigger paycheck may have more tax withheld that week, but it is the same tax brackets, and any over-withholding comes back in your refund. (Tipped workers: see our No Tax on Tips calculator.)

Is this my take-home pay?

No — this calculator shows gross pay, before taxes and deductions. To see what actually lands in your bank account, use the free HustleCalc take-home calculator.
